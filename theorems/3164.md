# Theorem 3164

Suppose

\[
-3 < \lambda < 3.
\]

Then

\[
\langle r\rangle^\lambda \in A_2(\mathbb{R}^3).
\]

## Proof

Let \(B(x_0,R)\) be an arbitrary ball. We consider

\[
\left(
\frac{1}{|B|}
\int_B \langle r\rangle^\lambda\,dx
\right)
\left(
\frac{1}{|B|}
\int_B \langle r\rangle^{-\lambda}\,dx
\right).
\]
